Aviator

A simple flight simulation where users can virtually pilot a space-shuttle–style aircraft. The experience focuses on the feeling of controlling and flying the aircraft in a virtual environment, with a basic interface and flight controls and without complex settings or customization options.

Intelligence behind the adventure

Smart operations

Connected systems help the park manage visitor flow, maintenance, information and safety.

📡

IoT and Sensors

Sensors track crowd movement to reduce wait times and improve safety. Smart lighting and air conditioning turn on or off based on motion or weather.

📱

Mobile Apps

Get real-time ride wait times, maps and bookings. The app also supports cashless payments and digital tickets.

🛠️

AI-Powered Maintenance

AI-based predictive maintenance helps identify ride maintenance needs before breakdowns, keeping operations smooth and reducing downtime.

🛡️

Smart Security

Face recognition at entry points is proposed for safety and easier check-ins, alongside emergency response systems connected to a central control point.

📊

Data-Driven Insights

Visitor-behaviour analysis helps improve the park layout, food options and ride efficiency.

A greener galaxy

Sustainability at Cosmic Quest

Our planned sustainability features focus on using resources thoughtfully and reducing the park's environmental impact.

☀️

Renewable Energy Sources

Solar panels are planned to power rides and lighting.

♻️

Waste Management Systems

Smart bins sort and compress waste automatically, with recycling stations placed around the park.

💧

Water Conservation

Water rides use recycled and treated water. Smart irrigation for gardens helps reduce water usage.

🏢

Green Buildings

Eco-friendly construction materials, energy-efficient LED lighting and efficient air-conditioning systems are part of the plan.

🚲

Eco-friendly Transport

Electric shuttle trains and bike rentals inside the park encourage lower-impact travel, alongside public transport and carpooling.

More ways to enjoy the galaxy

Discover imaginative food, personalized keepsakes and a digital passport that celebrates every stop on your journey.

Galactic Cuisine

Glow-in-the-Dark Drinks: Special drinks glow under blacklights for a fun example of science in action.

Space Rations and Molecular Gastronomy: Try astronaut-inspired treats such as freeze-dried ice cream, plus creative foods where chefs use chemistry to make surprising experiences.

Robotic Servers: Friendly robots take orders and serve meals in the food courts.
